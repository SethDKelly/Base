# Phase 002 — Concept Discovery, Candidate Inventory & Divergent Exploration

## Role in the lifecycle

Phase 002 begins explicit concept discovery. It explores alternative conceptual decompositions while keeping the work divergent enough to avoid mistaking the first plausible feature or domain model for the final concept system.

## Methodological intention

Discover candidate concepts that may fulfill the purposes established in Phase 001, treating concepts as coherent, user-facing, semantic units of behavior rather than implementation modules, screens, database entities, or feature-list labels.

## Primary design questions

- What candidate concepts could fulfill the identified purposes?
- Which candidates correspond to mental constructs users can understand and behavioral units that can be specified independently?
- What alternative decompositions should be explored before convergence?
- Which candidates appear overloaded, fragmentary, derivative, implementation-shaped, or insufficiently purposive?
- Where might familiar or generic concepts already exist?
- What uncertainties should remain open for later specification and boundary testing?

## Prerequisites

Phase 001 must provide sufficiently clear purpose, need, context, and success framing to evaluate candidate concepts meaningfully.

## Expected durable outputs

Typically includes candidate concept inventory, candidate-to-purpose associations, alternative decomposition hypotheses, rejected or deferred candidates with rationale where useful, candidate boundary questions, suspected genericity or familiarity opportunities, and uncertainty/convergence criteria for Phase 003.

## Explicit exclusions

This phase must not treat candidate names as final specifications, define implementation entities or modules, or prematurely force all candidates into a complete application architecture.

## Entry criteria

Purpose framing is sufficiently established to distinguish concept discovery from unconstrained feature brainstorming.

## Exit criteria

The phase may exit when there is a broad enough candidate space to support reasoned convergence; major alternative decompositions have been considered; obviously implementation-shaped or non-concept candidates are identified; and the retained candidates are ready for behavioral specification rather than assumed complete.

## Control structure

The phase begins with a mandatory `002-A` start gate, which derives the project-specific discovery and convergence subphases. The final subphase is the Phase 002 consolidation, exit review, and handoff.
