# Phase 001 — Purpose, Context, Need & Success Framing

## Role in the lifecycle

Phase 001 is the first Jackson-aligned concept-design phase. It converts the project-intake framing into an explicit account of the human purposes and contextual needs the design must serve before candidate concepts are proposed.

## Methodological intention

Establish a purpose-centered basis for design so that later concepts are justified by meaningful needs rather than by inherited features, implementation structures, or solution assumptions.

## Primary design questions

- What meaningful improvement should the product create?
- For whom, in what context, and under what relevant conditions?
- What burdens, constraints, failures, or unmet needs motivate the design?
- What purposes belong to the overall application, particular actors, or later candidate concepts?
- What representative success situations would demonstrate that those purposes are being served?
- What tensions, conflicting purposes, uncertainties, or likely misfits must remain visible?

## Prerequisites

Phase 000 must have produced a sufficiently understandable project definition, scope, actor/stakeholder framing, constraints, assumptions, and evidence posture.

## Expected durable outputs

Typically includes authoritative current knowledge for:

- application/product purpose framing;
- actor or stakeholder purposes where relevant;
- need and burden statements;
- desired outcomes and success framing;
- contextual conditions that materially affect design;
- tensions, uncertainties, and purpose conflicts;
- traceability from intake evidence to purpose statements.

## Explicit exclusions

This phase must not freeze a concept catalog, feature decomposition, interface structure, architecture, data model, API, service topology, or implementation plan.

## Entry criteria

Phase 000 has passed and the design problem is sufficiently framed to investigate purposes without relying on hidden assumptions.

## Exit criteria

The phase may exit when the principal purposes and needs are explicit enough to judge future concept candidates; success framing is concrete enough to expose weak or irrelevant solutions; important tensions and uncertainties are visible; and no candidate solution has been prematurely made authoritative.

## Control structure

The phase begins with a mandatory `001-A` start gate. That gate reviews this intention and derives the dependency-safe substantive subphases required by the cloned project. The final subphase is the Phase 001 consolidation, exit review, and handoff.

## Implementation state

- **Readiness:** not ready
- **Execution:** not started
- **Authorization:** not yet
